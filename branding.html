03

Logo Usage

The logo is always used at its native navy color or inverted to cream on dark backgrounds. Never recolor, stretch, or place on a busy background.

✓ On Cream Background Preferred — navy logo on warm cream
✓ On White Background Acceptable for print or document use
✓ On Navy Background Invert to cream/white using CSS filter or export a white version

Clear Space: Always maintain a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "AH" letterforms.
Minimum Size: Never display the logo smaller than 32px tall on screen or 0.4" in print.
Don't: Rotate, recolor, add drop shadows, place on patterned or busy backgrounds, or use the AH initials without the full name lockup (except in favicon/icon contexts).

04

Tone of Voice

Copy should feel like the person who wrote it — confident, precise, and warm without being effusive. The brand voice mirrors the qualities of a great EA: calm authority, no fluff, genuine care.

Confident

State things directly. Avoid hedging language like "I try to" or "I hope to." You know what you bring.

Discreet

Professional and measured. Nothing showy. The work speaks; the brand doesn't need to shout.

Warm

Human, not corporate. Short sentences with personality. Approachable without being casual.

Precise

Every word earns its place. No filler phrases, no padding. Be specific where it counts.

Avoid: Overpromising

"I'll handle everything!" — too salesy. Speak to judgment and partnership, not task completion.

Avoid: Jargon

Skip buzzwords like "synergy," "bandwidth," or "value-add." Write the way a smart person talks.
